This is the independent introduction website, separate from the stu-mcp Python/MCP project.

- Keep the repository private until the owner explicitly requests otherwise. Develop locally; do not deploy.
- Side Quest Adoption Center is the retained design baseline. Preserve its warm OKLCH palette, illustration language, spacing, rounded controls and motion. The original is in reference/side-quest/.
- Prefer real ReactDrops components where they improve the experience. Preserve their Component License and third-party notices. Do not redistribute a standalone component library.
- Product facts come from the public stu-mcp repository and verified documentation. Demo interactions copy prompts; they do not query or authenticate a user's account.
- Do not read or copy credentials, private school_hub or messaging data. Never change the sibling stu-mcp repository as part of website work.
- Check desktop and 390px mobile, keyboard/touch, horizontal overflow, console errors and reduced motion before delivery.
- Preserve stage screenshots and document the source and actual scope of verification.
